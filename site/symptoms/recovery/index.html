Recovery and discomfort

Know what recovery claims are built on.

BPC-157 and TB-500 feature in many online recovery conversations. Experimental findings do not establish that these products safely heal an injury in people. Persistent pain or a change in function deserves a proper assessment. [3, 4] (sources)
