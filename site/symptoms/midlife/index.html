Midlife changes

Several changes deserve one thoughtful conversation.

The menopausal transition can affect sleep, temperature regulation, sexual comfort, and concentration. Changes in weight and body composition can also occur around this stage of life. A broader assessment helps you discuss the concerns together. [13] (sources)

Peptide research is one part of a much larger health conversation. Femalin does not present peptides as a way to reverse menopause, restore fertility, or replace individualized menopause care.

Relevant reading

Weight-management medicines where clinically appropriate; topical GHK-Cu for skincare context. Neither is a general treatment for menopause.

Ask your clinician

  • Which of my symptoms may be related?
  • What established hormonal or nonhormonal options should we discuss?
  • What else needs assessment?
