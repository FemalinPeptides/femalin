Skin changes

A closer look at peptides in skincare.

Topical copper peptides appear in skincare products and have been studied in specific settings. Results depend on the formulation and use studied; evidence for one product does not validate every serum. Topical cosmetic use also does not establish the safety of injectable GHK-Cu. [4, 11] (sources)
