A clearer view of the science

Some peptide medicines have approved uses. Some peptides appear in topical cosmetics. Many popular compounds remain experimental for the goals discussed online. Femalin keeps these distinctions visible, so popularity never stands in for proof. [4–9] (sources)

Understand the evidence labels
  • Prescription medicineA specific product has an approved indication in a named jurisdiction.
  • Cosmetic contextA topical ingredient is being discussed in a cosmetic setting.
  • Other clinical contextThe history or setting needs explanation.
  • Experimental researchThe wellness goal discussed is not established.
