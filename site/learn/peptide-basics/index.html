Peptide basics

What a peptide name can and cannot tell you

A peptide name identifies a molecule. It does not tell you that every product containing it is approved, that every advertised use is supported, or that it suits you. Start by asking what exact product is being discussed, for which purpose, and in which country. [4–6] (sources)

Then look at the evidence. A change in a laboratory marker is different from a meaningful improvement in symptoms. A result in one population does not automatically apply to another. Knowing those differences makes a profile more useful than a list of promised benefits.
