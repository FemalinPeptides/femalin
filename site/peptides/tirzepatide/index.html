Tirzepatide and metabolic health

Tirzepatide · also known as Zepbound, Mounjaro

Jurisdiction referenced United States (prescribing information for Zepbound)

Tirzepatide acts on GIP and GLP-1 receptors. Certain branded products have approved uses for eligible patients, including weight management; suitability depends on the product label and individual assessment. [8] (sources)

Common side effects include gastrointestinal symptoms. The US Zepbound label includes a thyroid C-cell tumour boxed warning and contraindications involving medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2. Other important risks include pancreatitis and gallbladder problems. Tirzepatide can affect oral hormonal contraceptive effectiveness: the US label advises a non-oral method or added barrier protection for four weeks after starting and after each dose increase. Review pregnancy plans and full prescribing information. [8] (sources)
