AOD-9604

Experimental for the wellness goals discussed here

AOD-9604 is discussed in weight-management marketing. Femalin does not present it as an established treatment for abdominal fat or as an equivalent to approved weight-management medicines. Review the evidence limits before drawing conclusions. [2, 4] (sources)

What this label means

Experimental research The wellness goal discussed is not established. A mechanism, animal result, small study, or testimonial cannot settle effectiveness and safety for you.
