Sermorelin beyond the wellness headlines

Sermorelin · also known as Geref

Context label. Historical clinical drug context; current product and jurisdiction require verification.

Sermorelin has a history of US drug approvals as Geref. Those approvals were withdrawn after the products were discontinued; the FDA later determined that the discontinuation was not for safety or effectiveness reasons. That history does not mean a present-day compounded formulation is FDA-approved. [10] (sources)

Modern advertising often makes much broader claims about energy, body composition, sleep, and ageing. Historical drug use does not establish those outcomes for healthy women. Ask a qualified clinician to explain the evidence for the proposed use and the legal status of the exact product.

Question to bring

What diagnosis are we treating, what evidence supports the proposed use, and what are the risks and monitoring requirements?
