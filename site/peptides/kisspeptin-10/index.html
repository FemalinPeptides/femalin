Reproductive signalling deserves careful interpretation.

Kisspeptin-10 · also known as kisspeptin 10

Experimental for the wellness goals discussed here

Kisspeptin research includes studies of sexual processing and reproductive hormones. A small trial using kisspeptin-54 in premenopausal women does not validate commercial kisspeptin-10 products or establish treatment of menopause, infertility, or low desire. Different forms are not interchangeable. [12] (sources)

What this label means

Experimental research The wellness goal discussed is not established. A mechanism, animal result, small study, or testimonial cannot settle effectiveness and safety for you.
